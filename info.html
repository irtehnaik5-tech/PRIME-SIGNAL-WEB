❓ Frequently Asked Questions
What is PrimeSignal AI? ▼
PrimeSignal AI is an AI-powered trading signal platform that analyzes market data in real-time and provides clear buy/sell signals with entry prices, take-profit, and stop-loss levels. It covers crypto (100+ pairs), stocks (100+), and forex (7 pairs).
How accurate are the signals? ▼
Our AI has an average accuracy rate of 78-85% based on historical data, using technical indicators like RSI, MACD, ADX, and Bollinger Bands. Trading always carries risk, and we recommend using proper risk management.
Is PrimeSignal AI free to use? ▼
Yes! PrimeSignal AI offers free access to all features. You get 3 free credits to start. Each Pair or Chart analysis costs 1 credit. The "Ask AI" feature is always free. You can earn more credits by claiming daily rewards (7-day streak gives +7 bonus credits).
How do I get started? ▼
Simply click the "Log in with Telegram" button on our main page, connect your Telegram account, and you'll instantly get access to AI-powered trading signals. You'll receive 3 free credits to start analyzing.
What assets do you cover? ▼
Currently, we cover 100+ crypto pairs (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, etc.), 100+ stocks (AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, etc.), and 7 forex pairs (EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, etc.). We're continuously expanding our coverage.
How often are signals updated? ▼
Spot signals are scanned every 5 minutes. Stocks and forex are scanned every 2 hours. You can also click the "Analyze" button at any time for a fresh signal (costs 1 credit).
What is the credit system? ▼
Each analysis (Pair or Chart) costs 1 credit. You start with 3 free credits. Claim daily rewards to earn more: 1 credit per day, and a bonus of 7 credits when you reach a 7-day streak. You can also purchase credit packs starting from $5.
What is the PRO version? ▼
PRO users get access to the Chart AI feature, which provides structured analysis with entry, stop-loss, and take-profit levels. PRO status can be unlocked by making a deposit (starting from $20) or purchasing credit packs.
